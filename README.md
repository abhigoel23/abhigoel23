# Abhishek Goyal

I build mobile products from zero to shipped, and own every step in between.

Mobile engineer with 13 years on Android and iOS, owning products end to end. Most recently I designed and built HelperBook, a fully offline household-payroll app, and took it to Play Store closed beta. Before that I spent over four years as Head of Mobility at Aim North Technologies, taking the Pulse inspection platform from an early prototype to 100+ B2B enterprise clients.

I work across native Android (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose), native iOS (Swift, SwiftUI), Kotlin Multiplatform and React Native, choosing the stack for the product and team rather than my own preference.

## What I build

- **MVP builds** — an idea taken to a released app, with the architecture and data model (often offline-first) decided up front.
- **App rescue and modernisation** — stabilising apps that have become hard to change or release, then modernising in shippable steps.
- **Kotlin Multiplatform migrations** — sharing business logic and data between Android and iOS, one module at a time.
- **Fractional mobile lead** — part-time ownership of architecture, releases and code review for teams that need senior judgement without a full-time hire.
